Start here: plan your next semester

  1. Completed — choose Completed, then tap every course you passed (check DegreeWorks). Transfer credit counts.
  2. Enrolled — choose Enrolled, then tap the courses you are taking this semester.
  3. Planned — choose Planned, then tap the courses you want for next semester. Amber courses are eligible now. Press Confirm & Recompute.
  4. Send — open My Plan, type your name and W number, and press ✉ Email Plan. Do not change the email subject.
  5. Meet — book your advising appointment from the EAB Navigate email. Your plan must arrive before the meeting. Your Alt PIN is released after the meeting.
Substitutions: ENGR 4321 counts for CSEC 3355, MIS 3363 for CIS 3310, MIS 3362 for CIS 3362. Use the ⇄ button on a course (graph) or the ⇄ link (list) to pick the substitute.
